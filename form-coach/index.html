Live Form Coach

Your camera watches your body in 3D while you exercise, from the front, the side or any angle. It checks your posture on every rep, says out loud what to fix, and counts good and bad reps. Video stays on your device and is never uploaded.

1. Pick your exercise

2. Set up your camera

    3. Start

    Your browser will ask for camera permission. The first start downloads the body-tracking model (about 6 MB), so it needs internet once.

    This checks body angles, not injuries. If something hurts sharply, stop and see a doctor or physiotherapist.
